# Independent rehearsal review guide

Status: procedure, not a completed review. The author desk review remains useful but does not meet independence requirements.

## Reviewer task

Use a person or separately authorized reviewer session that did not author the candidate. Read the discovery fixture and acceptance requirements before the assessment or author commentary. A different model name alone does not establish independence. No reviewer agent is launched by this guide.

Review these files in order: [fixture](../rehearsals/agency-inquiries/discovery.md), [assessment](../rehearsals/agency-inquiries/assessment.md), [proposal](../rehearsals/agency-inquiries/proposal.md), [design](../rehearsals/agency-inquiries/technical-design.md), and then [author desk review](../rehearsals/agency-inquiries/review.md).

## Required checks

- Recalculate 140 baseline minutes, 50 proposed minutes and 90-minute reduction from the fictional assumptions.
- Distinguish capacity value from cash savings. Include the assessment when describing combined payback.
- Confirm exactly two customer business systems and a response draft field; no implicit third mailbox integration.
- Identify unknown authentication, stable-ID, upsert, subscription and retention requirements.
- Check that duplicates, changed-content replays, ambiguous writes and failures have proposed handling.
- Confirm no claim that runtime controls or operational evaluations passed.
- Confirm every final business choice and external action remains with its authorized owner.

## Record to complete

Reviewer identity and independence basis: pending
Candidate commit or exact artifact versions: pending
Review date: pending
Checks actually performed and evidence: pending
Findings with severity, location, consequence and requested fix: pending
Disposition: NOT REVIEWED

Use PASS for documentation coherence only when criteria are met; REVISE for defects and BLOCKED for missing evidence. PASS does not authorize production, sending, spending or customer delivery. Preserve findings and candidate versions; substantive revisions require another review. Do not sign another reviewer's name or fabricate independence.
