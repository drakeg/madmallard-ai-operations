## Creator Link Audit scope decision — November 16, 2026

Greg explicitly authorized a separate implementation repository and local Docker Desktop
runtime for this product. This does not activate agents or runtimes from the planning
repository. No other project’s private authentication is reused. The application repository
contains its working backlog, license/dependency review, test evidence and undeployed
ECR/ECS/EKS preparation.

Pilot: up to 20 owner-supplied descriptions/posts, 100 unique outbound links, manual
verification of up to 10 flagged source links, and one retest after approved corrections.
The $149 price is a hypothesis, not validated demand. HTTP success does not prove commission
tracking, intended product identity or discount validity. No automatic publication,
affiliate ID replacement, account scraping, video download, form submissions, outreach,
paid APIs, purchases or new schedules are authorized.
