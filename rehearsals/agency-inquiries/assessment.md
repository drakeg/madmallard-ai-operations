# Workflow assessment draft

Client: SYN-AGENCY-001, fictional. Service price hypothesis: $299. Not invoiced or sold. Source of all calculations: [discovery fixture](discovery.md).

## Recommendation

Prioritize inquiry capture and a response draft in the tracking record. It removes a repeated copying step while preserving owner judgment. Verify system access and interface capabilities before offering implementation.

| Rank | Opportunity | Rationale | Delivery estimate | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture inquiry and prepare response draft | Repeats 20 times per week in the fixture | 8–16 hours, unverified | Record completeness and owner minutes |
| 2 | Weekly open-inquiry summary | Helps review outstanding inquiries | 3–5 additional hours, separate scope | Time to review queue |
| 3 | Categorize service requests | May help routing if categories are stable | 4–8 additional hours, separate scope | Agreement with owner labels |

These estimates do not establish technical feasibility. Opportunities 2 and 3 are not included in the $750 pilot. Begin without an LLM: an approved response template may be sufficient. Add language generation only if a comparison demonstrates useful improvement after review cost.

## Illustrative benefit calculation

Current work: 20 × (4 + 3) = 140 minutes weekly.
Proposed work: 20 × 2 + 10 = 50 minutes weekly.
Potential reduction: 90 minutes, or 1.5 hours weekly. At $50/hour, that is $75 of capacity weekly, not cash savings or extra revenue.

Illustrative four-week monthly value is $300. At $20/month incremental cost, net capacity value is $280. A $750 implementation would take about 2.7 such months to offset, or about 3.7 months including the $299 assessment. These figures assume the baseline and reduction hold; no payback is promised. Real measurement must include review, exceptions and maintenance.

## Success and limits

Proposed success: a complete tracking record per valid submission, no duplicate records during replay tests, a response draft marked for review, and measured owner effort lower than the real baseline.
