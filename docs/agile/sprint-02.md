## Five prospect test design

Choose five small agencies using public business pages and existing contacts. Require a legitimate business contact route, evidence of inquiry intake and a plausible decision-maker. Do not infer manual pain from a website. Ask how the last inquiry moved between systems, how often copying happens, time required, existing automations, duplicate handling, access and budget. Record facts versus unknowns. No leads were collected or contacted in this sprint.

Go criterion for a separately authorized validation effort: one buyer accepts an assessment fee or an implementation deposit after scope and feasibility are verified. Revise after ten qualified conversations without a paid commitment. Avoid confusing compliments with buying intent.
