## Economic stress test

Use an illustrative owner-time value of $50/hour, not a wage claim. At a $750 pilot and assumed $40 direct costs, remaining contribution is $710 before owner labor. It covers 14.2 total owner hours at $50/hour before fixed overhead and tax. Delivery at 16 hours plus sales and review is therefore below that target. At 8 delivery hours plus 4 sales/review hours, contribution after the $600 time allowance is $110 before other overhead.

A sustainable quote must include all expected human time. Example: 16 delivery plus 4 sales/review hours at $50, plus $40 direct costs, produces a $1,040 floor before fixed overhead, tax and profit allowance. This is a pricing calculation, not a recommendation to guarantee a market price. Test the $750 pilot only with deliberately limited learning scope and an explicit labor cap; re-quote before commitment if feasibility exceeds it.

For care at $99 with an illustrative $10 allocated cost, the $89 contribution covers 1.78 total hours at $50/hour before overhead. A 30-minute support promise does not cap incident investigation, reporting and admin automatically. Track all of them. Do not sell broad maintenance under this fee.
